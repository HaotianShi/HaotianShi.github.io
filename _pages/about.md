I am Haotian Shi (石皓天), a Tenured Associate Professor at the College of Transportation Engineering, Tongji University. I received my Ph.D. degree in Transportation Engineering from University of Wisconsin-Madison. I have a multidisciplinary academic background spanning transportation engineering, computer science, and mechanical engineering, with degrees from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and Tianjin University, including a Ph.D. and M.S. in Transportation Engineering, an M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science, and an M.S. and B.S. in Power Machinery & Engineering.

My primary research interests include Driving Behavior Modeling & Prediction, Connected Automated Vehicle (CAV) Control, Traffic Management, AV Human-Machine Interaction, and 3D Scene Generation for E2E AV Testing. I have served as Principal Investigator (PI) on three projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), along with multiple other national- and provincial/ministerial-level research projects. I have published over 80 academic papers in top-tier transportation or computer science journals/conferences such as Transportation Research Part C/E、IEEE T-ITS、CACAIE、COMMTR、ECCV. Additionally, I hold more than 30 granted invention patents in United States and China.

I recruit multiple Ph.D. and Master’s students each year and welcome Postdoctoral researchers on a rolling basis, particularly those with backgrounds in transportation, automotive engineering, computer science, mathematics, and related fields. Students interested in autonomous driving, intelligent transportation systems, AI, and human-machine interaction are welcome to apply.

Office Room: Tongda Building A503, Jiading Campus, Tongji University.

主要研究方向包括自动驾驶行为建模与控制优化、端到端算法测试与场景生成、智能驾驶人机交互、智能网联交通系统等。主持国家自然科学基金3项（优青（海外）、面上项目、青年C类）等多项国家级、省部级项目。已发表学术论文80余篇，其中以一作/通讯在TR Part C/E、IEEE T-ITS、CACAIE、COMMTR等中科院一区TOP SCI发表近30篇。授权中美发明专利30余项。在ECCV、INFORMS、IEEE IV/ITSC、TRB等国际学术会议作学术报告40余次。入选国家级青年人才计划、上海市白玉兰人才计划（海外）、上海市晨光计划。获国家自然科学基金委指导的首届OnSite自动驾驶算法挑战赛高速赛段第三名（指导教师带队）、美国威斯康辛智能交通协会奖、SAE汽车智能交通技术创新奖一等奖、IEEE“塑造智能交通未来”一等奖等奖项。担任《GEITS》等一区期刊编委/青年编委，AAAI、ITFT等国际会议程序委员会委员，世界交通运输大会（WTC）智能云管云控、智能网联与车路协同控制、CAV政策法规、智能驾驶出行服务等技术委员会国际委员，中国公路学会高级会员。
